Independent Evidence Verifier

Checks a receipt's signature (was it forged or edited?) and, optionally, a piece of content's binding (does it still match what was sealed?). Runs entirely in this browser tab using the Web Crypto API — no data leaves your machine, and no EduAccess server call is required once you have a receipt file. See the note at the bottom for exactly what this does and doesn't prove.

What this proves: the receipt's signature is genuine (nobody forged or edited the receipt itself), and — if you pasted content — that content hashes to exactly what was sealed at generation time (nobody edited the artifact afterward).
What this does not prove: that the AI's output was factually correct, or that the lecture transcript it was grounded in was accurate. A valid receipt means "unaltered since sealing," not "correct."
